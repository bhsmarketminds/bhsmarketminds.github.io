Who We Are

Market Minds is a student-led nonprofit organization. We started as a high school economics club and grew into a broader program focused on improving financial literacy among younger youth. We want to make sure practical money skills are accessible to everyone early on.

Our Story

We noticed that many middle school students never get the opportunity to learn practical money habits like budgeting, saving, or understanding basic expenses before they hit high school.

That is why we launched our Youth Financial Literacy Program. Because we are students ourselves, we know how to explain these concepts in a way that is simple, keeps kids interested, and skips the dry lectures.

What Your Child Will Learn

We use simple language and interactive examples to help students understand four core ideas:

1

Needs vs. Wants

Learning how to prioritize essential items over things that are just fun to buy.

2

Budgeting

How to create a basic plan for money so they understand how to track spending.

3

Saving

Why putting money away early matters, and how to set a realistic goal to buy something they want.

4

Smart Spending

Recognizing daily sales tricks, comparing prices, and understanding choices before spending.

What Makes These Workshops Work

Practical Examples

We teach concepts through things middle schoolers actually care about, like cell phone plans or video games.

Interactive Activities

Instead of listening to lectures, kids play budget simulations and make real-time decisions.

Student-Led Environment

Being taught by responsible high school students makes the setting feel approachable and less like regular school.

How the Sessions Work

We don't expect kids to just sit and stare at a screen. Here is how we break up our time:

01

Quick Icebreakers: Simple, fast-paced games to get kids comfortable talking and collaborating with the group.

02

Real Examples: We explain concepts with stories and scenario cards that actually make sense to a 5th-8th grader.

03

Budget Simulations: Kids run through a game where they get a specific profile, manage an income, and make trade-offs.

04

Reflection & Goals: We end by letting them write down a small, actionable money goal they can practice immediately.
